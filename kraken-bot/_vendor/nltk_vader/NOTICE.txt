The bundled original VADER Sentiment Lexicon is by C.J. Hutto and Eric Gilbert.
The immutable nltk_data index identifies its license as MIT. The upstream MIT
copyright and permission text is preserved in VADER-MIT-LICENSE.txt.

Local changes to NLTK 3.10.3 are limited to the dependency imports and constructor
loader call. The adjacent pairwise helper is extracted unchanged from nltk.util.
The loader reads and verifies the bundled static UTF-8 lexicon, without NLTK,
corpus downloads, external resources or user-controlled resource paths. Scoring
source, constants, tokenization, negation, punctuation, idioms and the lexicon
bytes are otherwise unchanged. PROVENANCE.json pins the complete original
release, original module/helper, lexicon asset and license bytes.

Research citation retained from upstream:
Hutto, C.J. & Gilbert, E.E. (2014). VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for
Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Text. Eighth International Conference on
Weblogs and Social Media (ICWSM-14). Ann Arbor, MI, June 2014.

Compatibility is scoped to this original NLTK analyzer and pinned lexicon. The
previously deployed NLTK version and cached lexicon have not been inspected.
The original NLTK distribution is used only in separate offline validation
instrumentation; it is excluded from application imports and runtime dependencies.
